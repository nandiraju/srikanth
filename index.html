Professional Summary

Srikanth Nandiraju is a Silicon Valley technology executive and innovator with over 30 years of experience in artificial intelligence, machine learning, software engineering, and product design. He currently serves as Chief Technology Officer (CTO) at a life sciences startup based in Foster City, California, leading AI-powered platforms for single-cell multiomics cancer diagnostics.

Over his career, Srikanth has held senior technology leadership roles at eBay, PayPal, and Experian. He holds a Master's in Computer Applications and is the inventor on more than 6 approved United States patents.
